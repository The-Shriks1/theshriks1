## Avoided Overlaps

- `/services/ai-ml-systems` vs `/fleet/lokiai` — Service pages describe client delivery; LokiAI page describes the product. Different audiences and intents.
- Brand queries all funnel to `/` or `/facts`. Do not create separate brand pages.

- Long-term topical and entity authority strategy — not quick-win targeting.
